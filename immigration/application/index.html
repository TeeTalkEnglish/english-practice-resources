Part 1

Key vocabulary

Learn the words and phrases that often appear in visa application interviews.

01
Word / phrase Meaning Example sentence Hint
visa application The form or process you use to ask for a visa. “I submitted my visa application last month.” đơn xin visa
supporting documents Documents that support the information in your application. “We provided supporting documents to show our relationship is genuine.” giấy tờ hỗ trợ
evidence Information or documents that prove something is true. “The officer asked for evidence of our communication.” bằng chứng
genuine relationship A real and sincere relationship, not a fake one. “We tried to show that our relationship is genuine.” mối quan hệ thật
communication history Records of messages, calls, and contact between two people. “Our communication history shows we talk often.” lịch sử liên lạc
application A request or official form you send to an authority. “Everything in my application is correct.” đơn xin
submit To send something officially. “We submitted the application online.” nộp
provide To give something to someone. “I provided photos, messages, and travel records.” cung cấp
explain To make something clear in words. “I need to explain why I submitted these documents.” giải thích
proof Strong evidence showing that something is true. “We have proof of our wedding and our trips together.” chứng cứ
application history Your past applications and previous visa decisions. “I will explain my application history honestly.” lịch sử đơn xin
visa refusal A decision to refuse a visa application. “If this applies to you, explain what happened honestly and briefly.” từ chối visa
Part 2

What evidence would you show?

Choose the best answer. These are language-practice questions, not legal advice.

02
Scenario 1

Officer: “How can you show that you and your husband communicate regularly?”

Correct. Message and call history is a natural way to show regular communication.
Scenario 2

Officer: “What document can show that you are married?”

Correct. A marriage certificate is direct proof of the marriage.
Scenario 3

Officer: “How can you show that you have visited each other?”

Correct. Travel photos and boarding passes help show that you have spent time together in person.
Scenario 4

Officer: “How can you show that you live together?”

Correct. Living arrangements can be shown with an agreement, household photos, or shared bills.
Scenario 5

Officer: “How can you show financial support in the relationship?”

Correct. Financial records can help explain how you support each other financially.
Scenario 6

Officer: “What evidence would show that you have met each other’s families?”

Correct. Family photos and messages help show you have spent time with each other’s families.
Part 3

Build your answer

Use the pattern: Answer → Detail → Evidence.

03

Question 1

How do you keep in contact with your husband when you are apart?

Answer

We usually communicate by text messages and video calls.

Detail

We talk about our day, our plans, and when we will see each other again.

Evidence

We also have our message history and call history from the past few months.

Question 2

What documents did you provide with your application?

Answer

I provided my passport, our marriage certificate, and some photos together.

Detail

I also included our travel records and some messages that show our relationship is ongoing.

Evidence

These documents support the information in my application and explain our relationship clearly.

Question 3

How can you show that your relationship is genuine?

Answer

I can show that our relationship is genuine because we have spent time together and kept in touch.

Detail

We have photos, messages, and travel records from different times in our relationship.

Evidence

These records show that we are honest, consistent, and committed to each other.

Question 4

Have you visited each other?

Answer

Yes, we have visited each other several times.

Detail

We met in person during our holidays and also during family events.

Evidence

We have photos and travel records that show when and where we met.

Question 5

Has anything changed since you submitted your application?

Answer

There have been no major changes to our relationship since I submitted the application.

Detail

We are still together and we still communicate regularly.

Evidence

I can provide recent messages, photos, and updates to show that our situation is the same.

Part 4

Speaking practice

Answer aloud first. Then reveal the example answer.

04
Question 1

Why did you apply for this visa?

Example answer

“I applied for this visa because I want to live with my husband in Australia and build a stable life together. We have a genuine relationship, and I want to move to Australia with him and support our future plans.”

Question 2

What documents did you provide with your application?

Example answer

“I provided my passport, marriage certificate, photos of us together, and some documents showing our communication and travel history. I also included records that support the information in my application.”

Question 3

What evidence did you provide about your relationship?

Example answer

“We provided wedding photos, photos with our families, screenshots of our messages, and records of our video calls. We also gave documents showing that we have spent time together and have a genuine relationship.”

Question 4

How do you communicate with your husband when you are apart?

Example answer

“When we are apart, we usually text each other and have video calls. We talk about our day, our plans, and when we will see each other again. We also send photos and update each other on our daily life.”

Part 6

Follow-up questions

Officers often ask a follow-up question after your first answer. Stay calm and answer clearly.

06

Officer

What evidence did you provide about your relationship?

“We provided wedding photos, screenshots of our messages, and photos of us spending time together.”

Follow-up

Can you give me an example?

“For example, we have photos from our wedding and photos of us visiting family. We also have message records showing that we speak regularly.”

Officer

How do you communicate when you are apart?

“We usually text each other every day and have video calls several times a week.”

Follow-up

What do you talk about?

“We usually talk about our daily life, our plans, our work, and when we will see each other again.”

Officer

What documents did you provide with your application?

“I provided my passport, our marriage certificate, and some photos together.”

Follow-up

Why did you provide those documents?

“I provided them to support the information in my application and to explain our relationship clearly.”

Officer

Have you visited each other?

“Yes, we have visited each other and we have photos and travel records to show that.”

Follow-up

When was the last time you met?

“The last time we met was in [month/year], and we spent several days together with family and friends.”

Part 7

Final interview challenge

Think for a few seconds, then reveal the structure and example answer.

07

1. Why did you apply for this visa?

Answer structure

Answer → Reason → Evidence → Calm closing.

Example answer

“I applied for this visa because I want to live with my husband in Australia and build a stable future together. We have a genuine relationship, and I have provided documents such as our marriage certificate, photos, and communication records to support my application.”
