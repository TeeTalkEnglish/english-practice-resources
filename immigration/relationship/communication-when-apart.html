Relationship & marriage Lesson 07

Communication When Apart

Practise explaining phone calls, video calls, messages, emails and how you stay in touch when you are not together.

01

The situation

Describe how you stay connected.

Say how often you call, what apps or methods you use, what you talk about and how you communicate when schedules are different.

Communication patterns vary. Use your own real information.

Essential vocabulary

Words for keeping in touch.

01

phone / video call

We video call every evening.

02

message / text / email

We text during the day.

03

stay in touch

We stay in touch even when busy.

04

call history

A record of calls made.

05

every day

We talk every day.

06

several times a week

We call several times a week.

Questions you may encounter

Talk about contact naturally.

  • How often do you talk?
  • How do you communicate when apart?
  • When was your last video call?
  • What do you usually talk about?
  • How long are your calls?
  • Do you message each other during work?
  • How do you manage different time zones?

Grammar focus

Frequency makes your answer precise.

How often?

We call every evening. We video chat three times a week. We usually message during lunch.

Role-play

Say what, when and why.

Question

How do you communicate when apart?

Answer shape

We usually [method] [frequency]. We talk about [topics], and we [another method] when [situation].

Speaking challenge

Describe your last conversation.

Say when it happened, how long it lasted, how you communicated and what you discussed.

Example only

Our last video call was yesterday evening. It lasted about forty minutes. We talked about our work, dinner and our plans for the weekend. We usually call every day and send messages when we cannot talk.

Keep it genuine

Real contact has a pattern.

This lesson is for English practice only, not legal or immigration advice.
