Daily Life Together

Practise describing normal routines, work, meals, cooking, shopping, chores, hobbies and weekends.

The situation

Small details make answers clear.

Describe what you usually do on a typical weekday and weekend. Mention who does what, and include one specific detail.

Every couple has a different routine, including couples who currently live apart.

Everyday vocabulary

Talk about ordinary life.

Questions you may encounter

Practise your normal routine.

  • What do you usually do together?
  • Who usually cooks?
  • What do you do after work?
  • What do you usually do on weekends?
  • Who does the shopping?
  • How do you divide the chores?
  • What does your partner enjoy doing?
  • How do you spend holidays together?

Grammar focus

Present simple for routines.

Use usually, sometimes and every

We usually eat dinner at seven. I sometimes cook. We go for a walk every Sunday.

Remember the third-person -s: My husband works. He cooks.

Role-play

Give a balanced answer.

Question

What do you do after work?

Answer shape

Usually, [routine]. My partner [routine]. In the evening, we [shared activity].

Speaking challenge

Describe a typical Saturday.

Include morning, afternoon, evening, meals and one hobby.

Example only

On Saturdays, we usually have a late breakfast. Then we go shopping and prepare lunch together. In the afternoon, my husband plays football while I visit a friend. In the evening, we cook dinner and watch a film.

Keep it genuine

Ordinary details matter.

This lesson is for English practice only, not legal or immigration advice.
