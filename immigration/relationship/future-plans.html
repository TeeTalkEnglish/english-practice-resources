Your Future Plans

Practise talking about where you plan to live, work and build your life together.

The situation

Describe plans without sounding rehearsed.

Talk about settling in, finding work, living together, buying a home, starting a family or other real hopes.

Plans can change. Use language that matches how certain you really are.

Future vocabulary

Language for what comes next.

01

plan to / hope to

We plan to live together. I hope to find work.

02

would like to

We would like to buy a home.

03

going to

We are going to move in July.

04

will

We will decide after we arrive.

05

settle in

We want to settle in first.

06

find work

My partner hopes to find work locally.

Questions you may encounter

Talk about likely next steps.

  • Where do you plan to live?
  • What are your plans after moving?
  • Where will your partner work?
  • Where will you work?
  • What do you hope to do together?
  • Do you plan to buy a home?
  • How will you settle into your new community?

Grammar focus

Match grammar to certainty.

Plans, intentions and predictions

We plan to move in September. We are going to look for an apartment. We hope to settle in quickly. We will make decisions together.

Role-play

Connect a plan to a reason.

Question

What are your plans after moving?

Answer shape

First, we plan to [plan]. Then, we hope to [plan] because [reason].

Speaking challenge

Describe your next year together.

Include where you will live, work and one shared goal.

Example only

After moving, we plan to live near my partner's work. I am going to look for a job after we settle in, and my partner will continue working in IT. We would like to save for a home and explore our new area together.

Keep it genuine

Plans can be honest and flexible.

This lesson is for English practice only, not legal or immigration advice.
