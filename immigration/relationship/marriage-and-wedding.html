Talking About Your Marriage and Wedding

Practise describing an engagement, proposal, ceremony, reception, guests, traditions and marriage certificate.

01

The situation

Describe the day and what it meant.

Talk about your proposal, wedding date and location, ceremony, reception, guests, clothes, food and traditions.

Details differ between families and cultures. Use your own real information.

Essential vocabulary

The language of a wedding.

01

engaged / proposal

They became engaged after the proposal.

02

ceremony

The formal part of a wedding.

03

reception

The celebration after the ceremony.

04

wedding venue

The place where the wedding happens.

05

bride / groom

The people getting married.

06

guest / certificate

A guest attends; a marriage certificate records the marriage.

Questions you may encounter

Practise clear wedding details.

  • When and where did you get married?
  • Who proposed, and how did it happen?
  • Where was the ceremony?
  • How many guests attended?
  • What did you wear?
  • What food did you serve?
  • Did you follow any family tradition?
  • Where is your marriage certificate?

Grammar focus

Sequence past events.

Past simple

We got engaged in May. We held the ceremony at a garden venue. Our families celebrated with us.

Use past verbs for a finished wedding.

Add detail with because and so

We chose that venue because it was close to both families, so everyone could attend.

Role-play

Answer with a simple structure.

Question

Tell me about your wedding.

Answer shape

We got married on [date] at [venue]. We had [number] guests. After the ceremony, we [reception detail].

Speaking challenge

Describe your wedding in one minute.

Speak before revealing the example. Never use the example as if it were your own story.

Example only

We got married in a small ceremony at a local garden venue in 2024. Our parents, siblings and close friends attended. After the ceremony, we had a reception with dinner and music. We also signed our marriage certificate that day.

Keep it genuine

Your wedding has its own story.

This lesson is for English practice only, not legal or immigration advice.
