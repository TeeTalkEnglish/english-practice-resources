Proof of Relationship

Learn to explain what real documents and shared experiences show, rather than simply naming them.

The situation

Explain what a document shows.

Practise naming a marriage certificate, photographs, joint bills, lease, travel records, messages, call history, invitations, correspondence, shared expenses or financial support, then add context.

Use only genuine information. Never create fake evidence, exaggerate details or hide relevant facts. This lesson is English practice only, not legal advice.

Evidence vocabulary

Documents and records.

01

marriage certificate

An official record of a marriage.

02

photographs

Pictures connected to a real event.

03

joint bills / lease

Home records naming both people.

04

travel records

Bookings or records of shared travel.

05

communication records

Messages, emails or call history.

06

evidence / proof

Information that supports what you say.

Questions you may encounter

Practise explaining, not listing.

  • What documents can you provide?
  • What do these photographs show?
  • When and where was this photo taken?
  • Do you have a joint bank account?
  • What do your joint bills show?
  • How do your travel records relate to your relationship?
  • How often did you communicate when apart?
  • What evidence do you have if your finances are separate?

Grammar focus

Use this, these and that to give context.

Weak versus useful

We have photos.

These photos were taken during our trip to Sydney in 2025.

We have a bank account.

We have a joint bank account that we use for household expenses.

If you do not have a type of evidence, explain the real reason: We do not have a joint account because we keep our finances separate, but we share our household expenses.

Explain evidence

Use the four-part pattern.

Say what it is, when it happened, where it happened and why it matters.

Question

What does this photograph show?

Answer shape

This is a photograph of [event]. It was taken in [place] in [time]. [People] were there, and it shows [real context].

Question

Why do you not have a joint bank account?

Answer shape

We do not have one because [real reason]. We manage our finances separately, but we [real shared arrangement].

Speaking challenge

Explain three real pieces of evidence.

Choose a document, a photograph and a record. Say what each one shows. Do not use evidence you do not actually have.

Example only

Our marriage certificate records that we married in June 2024. These photos were taken during our trip to Sydney in 2025. We also have a lease with both our names on it, which shows that we have shared our home since March 2025.

Keep it genuine

Explain the truth clearly.
