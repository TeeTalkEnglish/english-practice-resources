Relationship & marriage Lesson 01

How We Met and Started Our Relationship

Learn to describe your first meeting, early conversations and important milestones with clear past and present-perfect English.

The situation

Tell the story in your own words.

Explain where and when you first met, who introduced you, what you talked about and how you became closer.

Use your own real information. These are language examples, not a story to memorise.

Essential vocabulary

Words for beginnings and milestones.

01

meet / first met

We first met at work in 2020.

02

mutual friend

A person both of you know introduced you.

03

start dating

We started dating a few months later.

04

become close

We became close after talking every day.

05

serious relationship

Our relationship became serious over time.

06

milestone / anniversary

Moving in together was an important milestone.

Questions you may encounter

Practise specific answers.

  • How did you meet your partner?
  • When did you first meet?
  • Who introduced you?
  • What was your first conversation about?
  • When did you start dating?
  • What was your first date like?
  • When did the relationship become serious?
  • What important milestones have you shared?
  • When did you first meet each other's friends?
  • How long have you been together?

Grammar focus

Past simple and present perfect.

Past simple for completed events

We met in 2020. My sister introduced us. We started dating in July.

Use a specific time with a completed event.

Present perfect for time up to now

We've been together for four years. We've shared many important moments.

Use for with a length of time and since with a starting point.

Role-play

Build a natural answer.

Question

How did you meet?

Answer shape

We met [where] in [year]. [Person] introduced us, and we talked about [topic]. We became close because [real detail].

Speaking challenge

Tell your relationship story in one minute.

Include where you met, your first conversation, when you started dating and one milestone. Speak naturally, then compare with the example.

Example only

We first met through a mutual friend in 2020. We talked at a dinner and discovered that we both enjoyed hiking. We started dating a few months later, and our relationship became serious after we supported each other through a difficult move. We've been together for four years.

Keep it genuine

Your real details are enough.

Answer honestly and naturally. This lesson is for English practice only, not legal or immigration advice.
