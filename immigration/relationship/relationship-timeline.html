The situation

Make the order easy to follow.

Choose the important events in your real relationship and connect them with time phrases.

This lesson is English practice only. Do not invent dates or details to match the example.

Build the timeline

First, then, eventually.

01We met
02We started dating
03We became serious
04We got engaged
05We got married
06We lived together
07We travelled together
08Our current situation
Linking phrases: first, then, after that, a few months later, eventually, at that time, since then, later, currently.

Questions you may encounter

Move between dates and duration.

  • When did you first meet?
  • When did you start dating?
  • When did you get engaged?
  • When did you get married?
  • How long have you been together?
  • How long have you lived together?
  • What has happened since then?

Grammar focus

When did...? How long have...?

Specific event: When did...?

When did you start dating? We started dating in 2020.

Continuing duration: How long have...?

How long have you been together? We've been together for six years.

Role-play

Join the events together.

Model

We first met in 2020. A few months later, we started dating. We got engaged in 2023 and married in 2024. Since then, we've lived together.

Speaking challenge

Tell your timeline without notes.

Use at least five linking phrases and finish with your current situation.

Example only

We first met in 2020. Then we stayed in touch as friends. A few months later, we started dating. Eventually, our relationship became serious and we got engaged in 2023. We married in 2024. Since then, we have lived together and travelled to visit family.

Keep it genuine

Clear order, natural voice.

This lesson is for English practice only, not legal or immigration advice.
