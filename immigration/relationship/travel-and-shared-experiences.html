Relationship & marriage Lesson 09

Travel & Shared Experiences

Tell a clear story about a trip, holiday, destination, hotel, flight and favourite memory together.

The situation

Turn a memory into a short story.

Say where you went, when you travelled, how you travelled, what you did and why the experience mattered.

You can describe a local day trip or a shared experience at home if you have not travelled together.

Travel vocabulary

Useful words for experiences.

01

trip / holiday

We took a short trip in spring.

02

destination

Sydney was our destination.

03

hotel / flight

We booked a small hotel and a flight.

04

itinerary

Our plan for the journey.

05

memory

My favourite memory is the coastal walk.

06

travelled together

We have travelled together twice.

Questions you may encounter

Describe what happened.

  • Have you travelled together?
  • Where did you go?
  • When was your last trip?
  • What did you do there?
  • Where did you stay?
  • What is your favourite memory together?
  • Did you have any travel problems?

Grammar focus

Use past verbs for the trip.

Past simple and sequence

We flew to Brisbane, checked into our hotel and visited the museum. After that, we had dinner near the river.

Use first, then, after that to guide the listener.

Role-play

Tell the story in four moves.

Question

What is your favourite travel memory?

Answer shape

We went to [destination] in [time]. First, we [event]. Then we [event]. I remember it because [reason].

Speaking challenge

Tell a two-minute travel story.

Include the destination, transport, accommodation, activities and one feeling.

Example only

Our last trip was to Sydney in 2025. We flew there and stayed in a small hotel near the harbour. First, we visited the Opera House. The next day, we walked along the coast and tried new food. My favourite memory is watching the sunset together.

Keep it genuine

Specific memories make stories vivid.
